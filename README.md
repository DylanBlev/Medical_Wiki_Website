# Medical Wiki Website
Project for Assistive Technology Class

This is project is designed to be a resource and provide users who may have medical conditions that require them to be at medical facilities more than the normal person, but have developed an anxiety or fear towards these facilities. This resource will give them the base knowledge of simple procedures that may occur while they are there to try and ease the idea of coming to the medical facility/hospital (such as regular check-ups, bloodwork, x-ray scans, etc).
