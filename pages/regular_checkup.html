Basic

    When going for a checkup with your pediatrician (e.g., family doctor) there can be an overwhelming amount micro-procedures that may seem scary, but is completely normal.
    Once the nurse is ready to begin the standard health checks, they will call you back and start with normal body measurements, such as, measuring your height, weight, blood pressure, and occasionally eyesight.
    Afterwards, you will be taken to private room where the doctor of the office will come in and perform more personal procedures checking heart, lungs, temperature (if symptoms are present), eyes, ears, throat, and reaction.
